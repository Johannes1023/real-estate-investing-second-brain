# Annual operating-cost playbook

## Summary

A repeatable process turns private source bills into one annual summary per unit.

## Steps

1. Identify the period and collect source bills in the private document store.
2. Confirm which cost categories and allocation methods apply to the actual agreement and law.
3. Calculate the unit share and compare it with the advances received.
4. Record the result, issue date, and document pointer in `OperatingCosts/<UNIT>_OperatingCosts_<YEAR>.md`.
5. Add any confirmed follow-up date to `deadlines:` and run `Update`.

The seven completed 2025 notes demonstrate both credits and payments due. [U01](../OperatingCosts/U01_OperatingCosts_2025.md) is a credit example; [U02](../OperatingCosts/U02_OperatingCosts_2025.md) is a payment-due example.
